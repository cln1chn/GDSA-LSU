Art Submission

Starting the 2026–2027 school year, members will now be able to submit their own artworks of any medium to sell through GDSA. The club participates in many local art markets including Free Speech Alley, White Light Night, and more. Profits will be split 60% (You!) + 40% (GDSA) if you submit your work and volunteer to sell your work at the art market. Otherwise, the split is 50% + 50% if you submit your work and GDSA prints, mounts, and sells it for you.
