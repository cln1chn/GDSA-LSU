About GDSA

The Graphic Design Student Association at Louisiana State University was founded in the 1990's. Open to all students regardless of major, GDSA seeks to build and educate a community centered around graphic design. Through agency tours, competitions, portfolio reviews, speakers, workshops, and more, we strive to provide meaningful opportunities for members to grow as designers.

Meetings are held on the first Friday of each month at 1 p.m. in Julian T. White Hall Room 230. See you there!
