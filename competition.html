Design Competitions

With a monthly design brief, members have until the next meeting to come up with a logo for a fictional company. At the next meeting, members vote for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place. Prizes include:

1st. $50 giftcard of choice

2nd. $25 giftcard of choice

3rd. $10 giftcard of choice. All members are invited to participate—no experience required!
*Prizes subject to change.

How it works

  • A new prompt is announced at every monthly meeting.
  • Submit one single-page PDF containing your logo, your color palette, and one mock-up/application of the logo (eg. signage, t-shirt, waterbottle, etc.).
  • Submissions are due before the next general meeting using the Google Form linked below.
