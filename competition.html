Design competition

Every month, all members get the same brief. Anyone can enter — no experience required.

How it works

  • A new prompt is posted at the start of each month.
  • Submit one single-page PDF containing your logo, your color palette, and one mock-up or application of the logo in use.
  • Submissions are due before the next general meeting, using the Google Form linked under that month's prompt.
